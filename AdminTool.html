Oriental Admin Tool — Application Summary
About This Application

OrientalApplication is Oriental's internal tool for managing the company's purchase and procurement workflow, from the moment material is requested through to the vendor being paid for it.

A purchasing cycle starts as a Purchase Requisition raised for a project, is converted into a Purchase Order sent to a vendor, is tracked on arrival with an Outgoing Challan, and is closed out by recording the vendor's Bill and Payment. Alongside this core flow, the application also maintains supporting master data — Vendors, Items and Projects — and provides reporting and dashboard views across all of the above.

Purchase Workflow
Step 1 Purchase Requisition Request material for a project Step 2 Purchase Order Order the material from a vendor Step 3 Outgoing Challan Track material dispatch and receipt Step 4 Vendor Bill & Payment Bill the vendor and record payment
